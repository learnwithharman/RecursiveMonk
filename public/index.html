      🃏 How to Play UNO

      Match cards by color or number. First to empty their hand wins!

      🎯

      Objective

      Be the first player to get rid of all your cards. When you have 1 card left, shout UNO!

      🔄

      Playing Cards

      Play a card that matches the top card by color or number/symbol. Wild cards can be played any time.

      ⊘

      Skip

      The next player loses their turn.

      ⇆

      Reverse

      Reverses the direction of play. In 2-player games, acts as a Skip.

      +2

      Draw Two

      The next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn.

      ★

      Wild

      Choose any color to continue play. Can be played on any card.

      +4

      Wild Draw Four

      Choose a color AND the next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn.

      📣

      UNO! Call

      Press Call UNO! when you have 1 card. Fail to call it and another player can challenge you to draw 2 penalty cards!

      📖 Quick Start Tutorial

      Get up to speed in 4 easy steps

      1

      Create or Join a Room

      Click Create Room to start a game, or enter a 6-character code and click Join Room to join a friend's game.

      2

      Share the Link

      In the lobby, click Copy Invite Link and send it to friends. They'll land straight in your room.

      3

      Play Your Turn

      When it's your turn, click a card from your hand to play it. If you can't play, click the Draw Pile to draw a card.

      4

      Win the Game!

      Empty your hand to win. Remember to call UNO! when you have only 1 card left — or face a 2-card penalty!
